# 🍽️ RestroDine — Restaurant Intelligence & Weather-Aware Revenue Forecasting

An end-to-end Data Science and Business Intelligence project that transforms synthetic restaurant point-of-sale, workforce, customer review and weather data into actionable management insights.

RestroDine combines business performance analytics, revenue forecasting, workforce planning, customer intelligence and menu engineering in an interactive Streamlit application.

> **⚠️ Important — Synthetic Dataset:** All customers, transactions, products, employees, shifts, reviews and historical weather observations in this project are synthetic. They were generated for educational and portfolio purposes. The results demonstrate analytical methods and application functionality; they are not findings from a real restaurant.

## 1. 🎯 Business Problem

Restaurant managers need to make decisions across several interconnected areas:

- How much revenue might the restaurant generate over the next two weeks?
- How many employees may be required to meet expected demand?
- Are scheduled employees sufficient for forecast demand?
- What do customer reviews reveal about food, service and other restaurant aspects?
- Which products generate the most revenue and gross profit?
- Which menu items may require pricing, promotion or menu-placement decisions?

These questions are often investigated separately. RestroDine brings them together in one analytical application.

The project demonstrates how data preparation, statistical analysis, forecasting, operational planning and business intelligence can support restaurant management decisions.

## 3. 📊 Application Modules

RestroDine provides five analytical modules.

### 3.1 📌 Executive Overview

A management-level overview of restaurant performance, bringing important operational and financial indicators together.

### 3.2 📈 Revenue & Forecast

Analyses historical revenue and order performance and presents the 14-day revenue forecast.

Key capabilities:

- Historical revenue and order trends
- Revenue and order performance indicators
- 14-day revenue forecasting
- Forecast uncertainty intervals
- Calendar and weather-aware forecasting outputs
- Forecast refresh functionality

The forecasting model is based on Prophet. The saved model and its metadata are stored in the repository.

### 3.3 👥 Workforce Planning

Connects forecast demand with workforce requirements and operational scheduling.

Key capabilities:

- Forecast expected orders
- Estimate required staffing
- Compare required and scheduled employees
- Identify staffing shortfalls
- Review labour hours and labour costs
- Monitor labour cost as a percentage of forecast revenue
- Analyse employee capacity and shift allocation
- Explore the recommended workforce roster

**Revenue and workforce integration:** Workforce planning uses the expected revenue and demand generated by the forecasting pipeline to estimate staffing requirements, allocate shifts, calculate projected labour costs and identify staffing gaps.

The staffing and labour calculations are intended as operational planning tools, not a substitute for a validated workforce scheduling policy.

### 3.4 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Customer Intelligence

Turns customer feedback and purchasing behaviour into structured information for management.

Key capabilities:

- Customer review and rating analysis
- Aspect-level sentiment summaries
- Positive, neutral and negative feedback distributions
- Customer segmentation using Recency, Frequency and Monetary (RFM) analysis
- Identification of customer feedback themes and improvement areas
- High-value customer risk analysis

Customer experience analysis uses synthetic, pre-labelled aspect-level sentiment annotations. These labels provide structured descriptive inputs for analysing customer feedback themes and experience aspects. They are not predictions generated by a trained NLP model, nor independent evidence of real-world customer sentiment.

### 3.5 🍽️ Menu Performance

Evaluates menu products and their financial and operational contribution.

Key capabilities:

- Product sales volume
- Product revenue
- Product cost and gross profit
- Gross margin
- Revenue and profit contribution
- Menu engineering classifications
- Promotion and discount performance
- Product and category performance trends

Menu engineering uses four categories:

- **⭐ Star:** High popularity and high profitability
- **🐴 Plowhorse:** High popularity and lower profitability
- **🧩 Puzzle:** Lower popularity and high profitability
- **🐕 Dog:** Lower popularity and lower profitability

These classifications are based on project-specific thresholds. They are analytical guidelines rather than universal restaurant industry standards.

## 4. 🏗️ Technical Architecture

The project follows a structured analytical workflow, with revenue forecasting feeding directly into workforce optimisation.

```text
Synthetic Restaurant Data
          |
          v
    Original CSV Files
          |
          v
     Data Preparation
          |
          v
 Data Validation & Reconciliation
          |
          v
 Business Performance Analytics
          |
          v
    Revenue Forecasting
          |
          v
    Workforce Optimisation
          |
          v
 Customer Segmentation (RFM)
          |
          v
 Customer Experience Analytics
          |
          v
 Menu Intelligence & Engineering
          |
          v
   Precomputed Analytical Outputs
          |
          v
     Streamlit Application
          |
          v
    Management Decision Support
```

The analytical workflows are developed and executed separately from the Streamlit interface.

The application reads precomputed analytical outputs, saved model artifacts and metadata. It does not retrain the Prophet model every time a user changes pages or interacts with the dashboard.

The project also includes a MySQL database schema and a database-loading script for the relational data workflow.

## 7. ⚙️ Installation and Setup

### Prerequisites

- Python 3.11 (recommended)
- Git
- Conda or Python virtual environment
- A compatible operating system
- MySQL Server and MySQL Workbench (optional, for the database workflow)

### Clone the repository

```bash
git clone https://github.com/satish-shrestha-4u/restrodine-project.git

cd restrodine-project
```

### Create and activate the environment

Using Conda:

```bash
conda create -n restrodine-env python=3.11 -y

conda activate restrodine-env
```

Alternatively, using Python's built-in virtual environment:

```bash
python -m venv .venv

source .venv/bin/activate
```

On Windows:

```bash
.venv\Scripts\activate
```

### Install dependencies

```bash
python -m pip install --upgrade pip

pip install -r requirements.txt
```

## 8. ▶️ Run the Streamlit Application

From the project root:

```bash
streamlit run app.py
```

Streamlit will start a local server and provide a browser address, normally:

```text
http://localhost:8501
```

The application uses the analytical CSV outputs, saved model artifacts and metadata included in the repository.

No database connection is required to use the precomputed-output application workflow unless a particular feature explicitly requires it.

## 9. 🛢️ Optional MySQL Database Setup

The repository includes:

- `data/schema.sql`
- `load_database.py`
- Original CSV datasets

The database loader creates and populates the project database and performs integrity checks.

Start your local MySQL Server, then execute:

```bash
python load_database.py
```

The script prompts for the MySQL password.

> **⚠️ Important:** The loader drops and recreates project tables in the specified database. Use it only with a dedicated RestroDine database. Never point it at a database containing unrelated or valuable information.

The loader checks primary-key uniqueness, referential integrity, order and payment reconciliation, calendar and weather coverage, and labour-cost calculations.

## 11. ⚠️ Important Analytical Definitions and Limitations

### Synthetic data

All business data is generated for this project. No real restaurant, customer or employee records are represented.

### Gross profit

Gross profit is calculated as:

```text
Revenue - Product Costs
```

It excludes labour, rent, utilities, delivery fees and other operating expenses. It must not be interpreted as net profit.

### Weather data

Historical weather is synthetic and calibrated to western German climate patterns. Replacing it with real weather observations requires rebuilding the relevant analytical datasets and model inputs consistently.

### Customer aspect labels

Aspect-level sentiment analysis uses synthetic pre-labelled annotations. These provide structured inputs for descriptive customer experience analysis. They should not be interpreted as predictions from an independently trained NLP model or as real-world customer sentiment evidence.

### Forecasts

Forecast outputs are estimates based on the project's synthetic historical data and model assumptions. Prediction intervals represent model uncertainty and are not guarantees of future revenue or staffing requirements.

### Menu engineering

Star, Plowhorse, Puzzle and Dog classifications depend on project-specific popularity and profitability thresholds. Different restaurants may require different thresholds.

### Staffing recommendations

Staffing requirements are based on the project's forecasting and staffing assumptions. They are decision-support estimates rather than legally or operationally validated scheduling instructions. Real-world implementation would require additional consideration of employee availability, qualifications, labour regulations, rest periods and operational constraints.

### Business interpretation

The analytical results demonstrate how data-driven methods can support management decisions. They should not be treated as causal evidence or as guaranteed business outcomes.
